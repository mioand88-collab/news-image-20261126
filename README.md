A newspaper front page summing up Thursday 26 November 2026 in one image. The lead story is the Houthis' claimed strike on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery and Riyadh, which pushed Brent crude above $94. Around it: the US proposing new tariffs on 14 trading partners to tackle factory overcapacity; South Korea's central bank raising its rate to 3.25%; a fuel-tanker fire near Cairo that killed at least 30 people; S&P putting Nvidia's credit rating on downgrade watch after the turmoil over its CEO's exit and return; and Thanksgiving Day in the United States, where the 100th Macy's parade was postponed to Saturday by a storm — a first for weather. The footer adds Norway's top court voiding disputed oil licences, Kenya discharging its last Ebola patient, and North Korea's Kim calling for wider deployment of tactical nuclear weapons.

`main.py` (standard library only) copies the poster, `entry.png`, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The poster itself is drawn by `draw_poster.py` with Pillow — shapes, gradients and typography only, no photos or external assets.
